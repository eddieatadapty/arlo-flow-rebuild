Day 1

Pass the campaign into Adapty

The us.arlo.com order already carries the Meta campaign. Attach it (or the device model, for Amazon buyers) to the account as a user attribute, and it picks the setup flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

Early Warning, moments and the doorbell get their own first screens; product and Amazon ads open by the device being paired.

Week 2

Judge each ad on plans, not cameras

Each ad’s flow against today’s, measured on plans started in the free month and what each install from that ad earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the same five ideas. We found no Apple Ads for Arlo in the US between 16 October and 16 November 2026, and no Meta ad that links to the App Store. One Early Warning ad links straight to your plans page, selling the plan before there is a camera; in the app the same promise can wait until the camera is up. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
